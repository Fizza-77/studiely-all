What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, session identifiers, and persistent identifiers such as Firebase UIDs, which we refer to collectively as "cookies" for this policy.

3
How Studiely uses cookies

We use cookies strictly for the purposes below. We do not use cookies for advertising, behavioural profiling, or any purpose unrelated to providing and improving our educational platform.

3.1 Strictly necessary cookies

Essential for Studiely to function — Firebase Authentication uses session identifiers to keep you logged in; cookies help detect and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity; session data maintains platform stability. These cannot be disabled without severely impacting functionality, and do not require consent.

3.2 Functional cookies

Remember your selected curriculum, subjects, study mode preferences, difficulty settings, and language or display preferences. We seek consent before setting non-essential functional cookies where required by law.

3.3 Analytics cookies

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with Studiely — page views, navigation, feature usage, and crash diagnostics (via Firebase Crashlytics), plus approximate country or region only, never precise location. Analytics data is anonymised or aggregated where possible, is not used for advertising, and for users under 13, analytics cookies are disabled by default unless verifiable parental consent is obtained.

4
Third-party cookies

Studiely uses Google Firebase, which may set its own cookies as part of the services it provides, governed by Google's Privacy Policy (firebase.google.com/support/privacy). We do not permit third-party advertising networks, social media platforms, or data brokers to place cookies on Studiely, and we do not share cookie-based data with third parties for their own purposes.

Studiely uses Google AdMob to serve optional rewarded video ads on the Free plan. For any account identified as belonging to a child under 13, AdMob operates in non-personalised, child-directed mode — no behavioural targeting, no cross-app tracking. We do not permit any other third-party advertising network to set cookies or trackers.

5
Subscription and payment cookies

Where Studiely processes payments directly through our website, using Stripe, Stripe may set necessary cookies to facilitate secure transaction processing. These are strictly limited to payment functionality and are not used for marketing purposes.

App store purchases (via Apple or Google) are processed entirely outside our website, and Studiely does not set or read cookies in connection with those transactions.

6
Cookie duration

Session cookies expire when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain for a set period; Firebase may use persistent identifiers to recognise returning users, retained only as long as necessary for their stated purpose.

7
Your cookie choices and controls

You can control cookies through your browser settings — blocking all cookies, deleting existing cookies, blocking third-party cookies, or setting preferences. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent Studiely from functioning correctly.

You can opt out of non-essential analytics by adjusting browser or device privacy settings, or by contacting privacy@studiely.com. Declining analytics does not affect access to educational features or paid services.

8
Children's cookie use

We do not use advertising cookies of any kind. For users identified as under 13, analytics cookies are disabled by default. We do not use cookies to build profiles of children, and we do not allow third-party advertising networks to set cookies through our platform. Where a child chooses to watch an optional rewarded video ad to unlock an extra generation credit, that ad request is served in child-directed non-personalised mode without behavioural tracking or persistent ad identifiers. Parents and guardians can contact privacy@studiely.com to enquire about or request deletion of cookie-based data linked to their child's account.

9
Legal basis for cookie use

Where applicable under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or other data protection laws, strictly necessary cookies are placed on the basis of our legitimate interest in delivering a functional and secure service, and non-essential cookies are placed only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. We do not treat continued use of the platform as implied consent for non-essential cookies.
