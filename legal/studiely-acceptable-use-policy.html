2
Permitted uses

Studiely is designed exclusively for educational and learning purposes, including:

  • Creating and accessing study notes, summaries, and AI-generated learning materials;
  • Taking quizzes and reviewing performance data;
  • Following personalised curriculum paths;
  • Managing student accounts (for parents, teachers, and institutions); and
  • Using Studiely to supplement, support, or enhance formal education.
3
Prohibited conduct
3.1 Harmful or abusive behaviour

You must not upload or share abusive, threatening, defamatory, discriminatory, obscene, or harassing content; bully, intimidate, or exploit any user, including children; or impersonate another person or organisation.

3.2 Illegal activity

You must not use Studiely unlawfully, infringe intellectual property rights, distribute malware, or facilitate fraud or deceptive conduct.

3.3 Privacy and data violations

You must not share personal information about other users — particularly children — without authorisation; harvest or scrape user data; use another person's account without permission; or attempt to identify anonymous users.

3.4 Platform integrity and security

You must not bypass authentication or security systems; reverse engineer the platform; use bots, scrapers, or crawlers; or attempt unauthorised access to restricted systems.

3.5 Misuse of AI features

You must not use AI-generated content (including from Nyla) for academic dishonesty, submitting it as your own work without appropriate disclosure; attempt to manipulate AI features to generate harmful or misleading content; or use AI-generated content as a substitute for verified professional or academic advice.

3.6 Commercial exploitation

You must not resell, sublicense, or commercially exploit Studiely's platform, content, or services without written authorisation, or use Studiely to advertise or conduct commercial activities unrelated to educational use.

4
Content standards

Content you submit must be relevant to your educational activities, free of abusive or discriminatory language, free of third-party personal information shared without consent, and compliant with applicable law. We may review, moderate, or remove content that violates this AUP, without prior notice.

5
Children's use and parental responsibility

Children may only access Studiely with appropriate parental or guardian consent. Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising their child's use and for activity under the child's account. Studiely does not permit children to share personal information publicly or with other users. Report any concerning use to hello@studiely.com immediately.

6
Institutional and teacher responsibilities

Teachers, schools, and institutions are responsible for ensuring their students use the platform in accordance with this AUP, confirming that appropriate parental consents have been obtained, reporting suspected violations promptly, and using administrative tools solely for legitimate educational management.

7
Reporting violations

Report any suspected violation, especially anything involving children, to hello@studiely.com. We investigate all reports promptly.

8
Consequences of violations

Violations may result in suspension or termination of your account, removal of content, restriction of access, reporting to authorities where required by law, and legal action where applicable. We determine, in our sole discretion, whether conduct violates this AUP, with reference to the safety of all users, particularly children.
