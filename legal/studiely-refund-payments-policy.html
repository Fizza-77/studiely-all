4
How generation credits work

Credits are pooled for your plan period rather than released monthly, and you may use them at any time within that period. Credits do not carry over — any unused credits expire when the period ends. Every feature is available on every paid plan; only the number of credits and the price differ.

5
Exam Practice add-on

Exam Practice is a separate subscription from the main plan, priced at $29.99 USD per month. It can be added regardless of which main plan you hold, and is billed and renews independently of your main subscription. Cancelling your main plan does not cancel Exam Practice, and cancelling Exam Practice does not cancel your main plan — each is managed separately from your account page.

6
Auto-renewal

Paid plans and the Exam Practice add-on renew automatically at the end of each billing cycle, at the then-current price, unless cancelled before the renewal date. You consent to this recurring charge when you subscribe. For plans with a term longer than one month, we will email a reminder at least 7 days before renewal, stating the renewal date, amount, and how to cancel.

7
Cancelling

Cancel any subscription at any time from your account page, or by emailing support@studiely.com. Cancellation stops future billing; your plan and any remaining credits stay available until the end of the period already paid for.

8
Refunds

Every new account receives 5 free generation credits with no card required, so you can evaluate the platform before paying. Because of that, once a billing period has begun, the fee for that period is not refundable.

Where circumstances warrant it, we may offer a partial refund or an account credit at our discretion. Contact support@studiely.com and we will consider your situation.

This policy does not limit your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. If our service is not supplied with due care and skill, or is not as described, you may be entitled to a remedy under that law regardless of anything in this policy.

9
Failed payments

If a payment fails at renewal, we will attempt to notify you and may retry the charge. If payment is not collected, your account moves to the Free plan and any saved learning materials are retained.

10
Purchases made in the app

Purchases made inside our iOS or Android apps are billed by Apple or Google, not by Skyen Systems Pty Ltd. Refunds for those purchases are governed entirely by Apple's or Google's own policies — contact them directly. We cannot issue a refund for an app-store purchase from this website.

11
Billing disputes

Contact support@studiely.com with your account email, transaction details, and a description of the issue. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
