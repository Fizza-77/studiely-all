5
Children and parental accounts

Children under 13 cannot create their own Studiely account. A parent or legal guardian must create and manage the account on their behalf. This applies as our standard operating model for all users, worldwide, and is not limited to any one country's law.

For users in the United States, this model is also how we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • No advertising to children
  • No behavioural tracking of children
  • Data collection limited to educational use
  • Verifiable parental or legal guardian consent required for children under 13
  • Parents may review, correct, or delete their child's data at any time
  • Parents may withdraw consent at any time

Parents and guardians may submit requests via privacy@studiely.com.

5.1 Age verification measures
  • Users must enter a date of birth at registration
  • Users under 13 cannot self-create an account; registration is redirected to a parent/guardian flow
  • Age validation is performed in real time and stored securely in Firestore
  • Clear messaging explains parental consent requirements
  • Under-13 accounts are only permitted with verifiable parental consent.
